We’re pleased that you’re considering applying for production access to the Blue Button API. We designed our production access process and [Terms of Service](https://bluebutton.cms.gov/terms/) to ensure that Medicare enrollee data is secure and that enrollees have the information to make informed decisions when sharing their healthcare data with third-party applications.

## Checklist

Complete these steps to get production access:

1. [Test in the sandbox](/quickstart/) Build and test your app with synthetic data  
2. [Implement OAuth with PKCE](/api-documentation/authorization/#proof-key-for-code-exchange-pkce-extension-usage) Full PKCE authorization flow working, including token exchange and refresh.  
3. [Handle pagination](/guides/handling-pagination/) Your app handles large EOB bundles  
4. [Build a consent flow](/guides/building-a-consent-flow/) Users understand what data they're sharing  
5. [Write a privacy policy](/production-access/how-to-write-privacy-policy/) Meets CMS requirements  
6. [Write terms of service](/production-access/how-to-write-terms-of-service/) Consistent with your privacy policy  
7. [Optimize your app](/guides/optimizing-your-app-for-production/) Compression, efficient queries, error handling  
8. [Review the detailed application requirements](/production-access/requirements/) Fine-tune those last elements before submitting your application  
9. [Apply and demo](/production-access/) Submit your application and demo for the Blue Button team

## Ready?

Start with the [detailed requirements](/production-access/requirements/), or if you've already got everything ready to go, jump straight to [applying for production access](/production-access/).
