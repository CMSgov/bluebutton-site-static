We’re pleased that you’re considering applying for production access to the Blue Button API. We designed our production access process and [Terms of Service](https://bluebutton.cms.gov/terms/) to ensure that Medicare enrollee data is secure and that enrollees have the information to make informed decisions when sharing their healthcare data with third-party applications.

## Checklist

Complete these steps to get production access:

1. [Test in the sandbox](/api-documentation/developer-sandbox.mdx) Build and test your app with synthetic data  
2. [Implement OAuth with PKCE](/api-documentation/authorization.mdx#proof-key-for-code-exchange-pkce-extension-usage) Full PKCE authorization flow working, including token exchange and refresh.  
3. Handle pagination (LINK: TBD, Handling pagination) Your app handles large EOB bundles  
4. Build a consent flow (LINK: TBD, Building a consent flow) Users understand what data they're sharing  
5. [Write a privacy policy](how-to-write-privacy-policy.mdx) Meets CMS requirements  
6. Write terms of service (LINK: TBD, How to write your terms of service) Consistent with your privacy policy  
7. [Optimize your app](/api-documentation/optimizing-your-app.mdx) Compression, efficient queries, error handling  
8. [Review the detailed application requirements](requirements.mdx) Fine-tune those last elements before submitting your application  
9. [Apply and demo](production-access.mdx) Submit your application and demo for the Blue Button team

## Ready?

Start with the [detailed requirements](requirements.mdx), or if you've already got everything ready to go, jump straight to applying for review (LINK: Application process).
