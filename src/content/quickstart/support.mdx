## Join the community

[The Google Group](https://groups.google.com/g/developer-group-for-cms-blue-button-api) is the best place to ask questions, share feedback, and connect with other developers building with Blue Button.

## Contact us

Email BlueButtonAPI@cms.hhs.gov for:

* Production access questions  
* Technical issues not resolved in the documentation  
* Privacy policy or terms of service review  
* Bug reports

The team responds to emails within 3 business days.

## FAQs NEW

**How do I get started?** Follow the directions on \[Quickstart: LINK TBD\] to make your first API call in 5 minutes.

**How do I get production access?** See \[Production access: LINK TBD\] for requirements, the application process, and what to expect in the demo.

**Why is my refresh token not working?** Refresh tokens are single-use. Each token exchange gives you a new refresh token. If you have already used it, the user needs to re-authorize. See \[Data freshness: LINK TBD\].

**Can I test with real data in the sandbox?** No. The sandbox uses synthetic data only \[LINK TO SYNTHETIC DATA: LINK TBD\]. Real data is only available after you receive production credentials.
